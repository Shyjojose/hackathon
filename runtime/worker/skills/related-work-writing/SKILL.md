# Related Work Writing Skill

## Citation Style

Use APA in-text citations: (Author, Year) or (Author et al., Year) for 3+ authors.

## Paragraph Structure

A citable paragraph must:
1. State what the paper does (1 sentence).
2. Connect it to the researcher's central claim (1 sentence).
3. Provide a direct quote with citation (1 sentence, ≤ 3 sentences total).

Example structure:
> [Author et al. (Year)] demonstrate [what the paper does]. This [supports / extends /
> threatens] [central claim] because [connection]. Specifically, they find that
> "[direct quote from paper]" ([Author et al., Year], p. X).

## Rules

- Every paragraph must include a direct quote. No paraphrasing as a quote.
- Do not republish more than 3 sentences from a single paper.
- Paragraphs are for the researcher's use only. Do not publish them on the public page.
- If the paper is a preprint (arXiv), note this: "(preprint, not peer-reviewed)".
